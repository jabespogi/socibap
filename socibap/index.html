OUR CHURCH

Rooted in the Word.
Growing in grace.

Welcome to Society Bible Baptist Church of Baliwag. We desire to be a church where the Bible is honored, Christ is exalted, and people are encouraged to grow in faith and service.

Our church is located in Baliwag, Bulacan. Whether you are visiting for the first time or looking for a church family, you are warmly welcome.
